How tuition with us works

Two simple paths, whether you're looking for a tutor or looking for work.

For parents & students

  1. Tell us what you need. Class, subject, schedule, budget and mode, through a short form.
  2. Take a free demo. We match a tutor to your requirement and arrange a demo class.
  3. Start learning. Happy with the demo? Classes begin on your schedule.

For tutors

  1. Register once. Share your subjects, classes, experience and expected fee.
  2. Pick an opening. New tuition requirements are posted regularly, each with a number.
  3. Get matched. Apply to the assignments that fit you and we handle the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. Every new student gets one free demo class with a matched tutor before committing to anything.

Which classes and subjects do you cover?

Nursery to Class 12, all major subjects including Maths, Science, English, Social Science, Economics and Statistics, for all boards.

Do you offer home tuition or online classes?

Both. Home tuition is available in Delhi NCR, and online classes are available everywhere.

How do tutors get assignments?

Register once as a tutor, then apply to numbered tuition openings as they are posted. We match you and support you through the process.

How much does tuition cost?

Fees depend on class, subject and schedule, typically from a few hundred rupees per hour. Tell us your budget and we will match accordingly.
